Django & DRF: why each layer exists

What problem Django solves and why you'd reach for it, then what Django REST Framework adds on top. The spine is one concrete question: plain Django's awkward mapping of HTTP verbs onto a single view, and how it gets cleaned up in three rungs: a function that branches, a class that names methods, a viewset routed as data. The second half goes into the machinery each layer leans on: the ORM's QuerySet, serializers, the generic-view ladder, and the authentication and permission gate. Sourced from Django's and DRF's own docs, exclusively.
